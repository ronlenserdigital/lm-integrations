About Us

Two techs. One standard: do it right.

L&M Integrations is an owner-operated low voltage company built on hands-on field work. We specialize in commercial IP camera systems, camera drops, PoE cabling, NVR and switch setups, and MDF room buildouts for properties across Virginia.

Because the owners are the installers, nothing gets subbed out and nothing gets rushed. The people who quote your project are the same people pulling the cable, terminating the runs, and standing behind the work after handoff.
